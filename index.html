About News Dieppe

News Dieppe is an open-source, privacy-first Progressive Web App (PWA) displaying daily scripture and contemplative quotes rendered as glowing, realistic bent glass neon signs.

No fonts, bitmaps, or pre-rendered canvases are used. Every character is drawn in pure SVG vector paths with physics-based glowing filters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is News Dieppe?

News Dieppe is a free, privacy-first web application presenting uplifting scripture and quotes in handcrafted SVG glass neon typography.

How does the neon rendering work?

Each glyph is defined by its physical centerline path, electrode boots, and glass curves. Real-time SVG filters simulate neon core ionization, halo bloom, and ambient light spill.

Is News Dieppe completely free to use?

Yes. 100% free with zero ads, zero subscriptions, and zero tracking. Licensed under the MIT License.

Can News Dieppe work offline?

Yes. The app shell and verse catalogs are precached by the Service Worker, giving you instant offline reflection anywhere.

Does News Dieppe track me?

No. News Dieppe does not use cookies, collect personal data, or run third-party tracking scripts.
